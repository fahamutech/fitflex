Section 1

Revenue Streams Overview

FitFlex Af operates a multi-stream revenue model designed to generate income from all active participants on the platform — gym members, gym owners, and independent trainers — while remaining competitively priced against the existing informal gym market in Tanzania.

Stream Source Mechanism FitFlex Revenue MVP
FitFlex Platform Pass Gym Members Monthly subscription sold by FitFlex Pass fee minus gym payouts Yes
Direct Gym Subscription Gym Members % commission on each transaction 10–15% per transaction Yes
Top-Up Roaming Fee Direct Sub Members Differential top-up to unlock multi-gym access Margin on top-up fee Yes
Trainer Booking Commission Gym Members % of each session fee 15–20% per session Yes
Marketplace Sales Gym Members Product sales margin Product margin Yes
Gym SaaS License Fee Gym Owners Monthly/quarterly platform fee Fixed license fee Post-MVP
In-App Advertising Fitness brands Ad placements to gym members Ad revenue Post-MVP
ℹ️
Note for Developers
Every financial transaction on the platform — whether a pass purchase, direct subscription, top-up, or trainer booking — must flow through FitFlex's payment processing layer. No payments should be handled outside the app without explicit owner-side mapping (see Section 3.3).

FitFlex Platform Pass — Tier Structure

The FitFlex Platform Pass is FitFlex Af's flagship product. It gives members access to a network of partner gyms across Tanzania on a monthly rolling basis. Tiers are differentiated by the number of gym visits per month and the tier of gyms accessible.

The prices above (TZS 60,000 / 120,000 / 200,000 / 350,000) reflect the finalised 4-tier model validated against competitor research and margin analysis. Visit caps are soft caps; 1 visit = unlimited same-gym access for the entire day. Basic tier is restricted to 1 gym per day; Pro and above permit multiple gyms per day.

Business Rule BL-001
A Basic Pass holder attempting to check in at a Mid-Tier or Premium gym must be shown an upgrade prompt, not an error. The system must surface the upgrade path clearly before denying access.
Section 3

Direct Gym Subscription

A Direct Gym Subscription is a member's individual membership agreement with a specific gym, processed through the FitFlex app. It is independent of the FitFlex Platform Pass and gives the member access to one gym only.

3.1 Pricing Control

Each gym owner sets their own Direct Subscription pricing (daily, weekly, monthly) through the Gym Owner portal. FitFlex does not set or cap these prices. The gym's pricing is displayed transparently to members in the app.

3.2 Commission Structure

Business Rule BL-002
For every Direct Gym Subscription paid through the FitFlex app, FitFlex deducts a commission of 10–15% from the transaction before remitting the remainder to the gym owner. The exact rate within this band is negotiated individually with each gym partner during onboarding.
Gym Payout = Subscription Fee × (1 − Commission Rate) Example: Member pays TZS 50,000 monthly direct sub Commission rate = 12% Gym receives = TZS 50,000 × 0.88 = TZS 44,000 FitFlex earns = TZS 6,000

3.3 Offline / External Subscriptions

A gym member may hold a direct subscription with a partner gym that was paid outside the FitFlex app (cash, bank transfer, etc.). This is permitted under the following conditions:

  • The external subscription does not automatically appear on the member's FitFlex profile.
  • The gym owner must manually map the external subscription to the member's FitFlex account via the Gym Owner portal.
  • Once mapped, the member gains QR check-in access to that gym through FitFlex.
  • FitFlex earns no commission on externally-paid subscriptions — they are mapped as a courtesy feature to incentivise gym owners to bring members into the platform.
⚠️
Dev Note — External Mapping
The external subscription mapping feature requires a gym owner-side tool: the owner searches for a member by phone/email, selects the subscription type, sets an expiry date, and confirms. The system creates a non-revenue subscription record linked to that member's account. This must be clearly flagged in the database as "external/unmapped" so it is excluded from commission calculations.

3.4 Coexistence with FitFlex Pass

Business Rule BL-003
A member may hold both a FitFlex Platform Pass AND a Direct Gym Subscription simultaneously. These are independent products. Holding both does not grant any additional discount or benefit beyond what each product offers individually.
Section 4

Top-Up Roaming Access

A member who holds only a Direct Gym Subscription (single gym access) may unlock multi-gym roaming — equivalent to Pro Pass access — by paying a top-up fee. This preserves their direct gym relationship while granting them access to the broader FitFlex network.

4.1 Top-Up Calculation Logic

Business Rule BL-004
The top-up fee is the difference between the Pro Pass monthly price and the amount the member has already paid for their direct subscription in the current billing period.
Top-Up Fee = Pro Pass Price − Direct Sub Amount Paid (current period) Example A: Pro Pass = TZS 120,000 Direct Sub paid = TZS 50,000 Top-Up Fee = TZS 120,000 − TZS 50,000 = TZS 70,000 Example B: Pro Pass = TZS 120,000 Direct Sub paid = TZS 130,000 (premium gym) Direct sub ≥ Pro Pass → Special flat activation fee applies (TBD) → Member receives Pro-equivalent roaming access

4.2 Top-Up Access Rules

  • Top-up unlocks Pro Pass equivalent access — Standard and Mid-Tier gyms, up to 30 visits/month across the network, with multi-gym-per-day access (excluding their home gym, visits to which are governed by their direct sub).
  • Top-up access is valid for the remainder of the current calendar month only. It does not auto-renew.
  • The member's direct subscription to their home gym remains active and unaffected by the top-up.
  • Visits to the member's home gym continue to be governed by their direct sub terms (unlimited if monthly, single-entry if daily).
  • Visits to all other network gyms via roaming count against the 30-visit monthly allowance.
Business Rule BL-005
When a direct sub member's monthly fee equals or exceeds the Pro Pass price, the top-up activation fee is a small fixed amount (to be determined — suggested: TZS 5,000–10,000). This compensates FitFlex for network access without penalising the member for already paying a premium gym's rate.
💡
Upsell Opportunity
The top-up mechanic is intentionally designed as a conversion funnel. Members who use top-up roaming 2+ times in a month should be shown a prompt: "You've topped up twice this month. Switching to a Pro Pass would save you TZS X." This nudge should be triggered automatically by the system.
Section 5

Trainer Booking Commission

Independent freelance trainers list their services on FitFlex Af at their own rates. FitFlex earns a commission on every session booked and paid through the platform.

5.1 Commission Rate

Business Rule BL-006
FitFlex charges a commission of 15–20% on every trainer session booked and paid through the app. The exact rate within this band is negotiated individually with each trainer during onboarding. The commission rate is deducted before the trainer receives their payout.
Trainer Payout = Session Fee × (1 − Commission Rate) Example: Member books 1hr session at TZS 25,000 Commission rate = 15% Trainer receives = TZS 25,000 × 0.85 = TZS 21,250 FitFlex earns = TZS 3,750 With Pro Pass 10% discount: Member pays = TZS 25,000 × 0.90 = TZS 22,500 FitFlex commission (15%) = TZS 3,375 Trainer receives = TZS 22,500 × 0.85 = TZS 19,125

5.2 Trainer Pass Discounts

Pro, Premium, and Executive Pass holders receive a discount on trainer sessions. This discount is absorbed by FitFlex — the trainer always receives their full negotiated rate minus the platform commission only. FitFlex does not pass the discount burden onto the trainer.

Pass Tier Member Discount Who absorbs the discount?
Basic None N/A
Pro 10% off member price FitFlex (reduced commission income)
Premium 20% off member price FitFlex (reduced commission income)
Executive 25% off member price FitFlex (reduced commission income)
Direct Sub (no pass) None N/A
🚨
Critical Dev Note — Trainer Discount Logic
The trainer discount must be applied at the member-facing price level, not at the payout level. The system must calculate: (1) discounted member price, (2) FitFlex commission on discounted price, (3) trainer payout = discounted price minus commission. The trainer's agreed base rate is NOT the floor — the payout is always calculated from the discounted member price.

5.3 Session Types

Session Type Location Price Set By Commission Applies?
Gym Session At a partner gym Trainer Yes
Home Visit Member's home/location Trainer Yes
Section 6

Gym Payout Model

Gyms earn revenue from FitFlex through two channels: commission payouts from Direct Subscriptions processed in-app, and per-visit payouts from FitFlex Platform Pass members.

6.1 Per-Visit Rate (Platform Pass)

Business Rule BL-007
Each partner gym negotiates its own per-visit rate with FitFlex on a case-by-case basis at onboarding. There is no platform-wide fixed rate. However, FitFlex must ensure the negotiated rate allows the Platform Pass to remain financially viable (i.e. total payout obligations must not exceed pass revenue in aggregate).

Indicative per-visit rate ranges by gym tier:

Gym Tier Indicative Per-Visit Range Rationale
Standard TZS 2,000 – 3,500 Matches or slightly exceeds their own daily pass rate
Mid-Tier TZS 3,500 – 6,000 Discounted vs their daily rate but high volume compensates
Premium TZS 6,000 – 12,000 Fully negotiated; must reflect their premium positioning

6.2 Payout Timing

  • Platform Pass per-visit payouts are calculated and disbursed monthly, on the 5th of the following month.
  • Direct Subscription commission payouts are disbursed weekly, covering the prior 7-day period.
  • All payouts are made via mobile money (M-Pesa Business, Airtel Business) or direct bank transfer to the gym's registered account.

6.3 Gym Owner Non-Revenue Incentives

Beyond financial payouts, gym owners receive the following platform benefits to incentivise sustained participation:

  • Real-time visit analytics — live dashboard showing member check-ins, peak hours, and attendance trends.
  • In-app promotions module — ability to create and schedule promotional messages broadcast to FitFlex app users, targeted by location, age, gender, and member type.
  • Digital platform presence — listed on a growing fitness marketplace serving Tanzania's gym-going population and fitness-conscious visitors/expatriates.
  • Member communication tools — direct messaging to their subscriber base via push notifications, SMS, and in-app messages.
Section 7

Subscription Lifecycle & Renewals

7.1 Renewal Model

Business Rule BL-008
All FitFlex Platform Pass subscriptions are auto-renewing. The member's registered payment method is charged automatically on the same calendar date each month (e.g. subscribed on the 10th → charged every 10th). Members must receive an advance notification 3 days before each renewal charge.

7.2 Renewal Notification Sequence

Timing Notification Type Message
T−3 days Push + In-app "Your [Plan] renews in 3 days for TZS [X]. Tap to manage."
T−1 day Push + SMS "Your [Plan] renews tomorrow. Ensure your M-Pesa balance is sufficient."
T=0 (Renewal day) Push + Email Confirmation of successful renewal with receipt.
T=0 (If failed) Push + SMS Payment failure alert with recovery options.

7.3 Expiry & Grace Period

Business Rule BL-009
When a subscription expires (due to non-renewal or cancellation), the member enters a 24-hour grace period. During this period, their access remains active but they receive renewal prompts. After 24 hours, gym access is fully revoked until the subscription is renewed or a new plan is purchased.

7.4 Cancellation Rules

  • Members may cancel at any time via the app. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
  • No pro-rata refunds are issued for early cancellation of monthly plans.
  • Daily pass subscriptions are non-refundable once activated.
  • Upon cancellation, the member retains access until the billing period ends, then enters the 24-hour grace period.

7.5 Payment Failure Recovery

  • On payment failure, the system retries once after 6 hours.
  • If the retry fails, the member enters the 24-hour grace period and must manually resolve the payment.
  • The app must surface clear recovery options: retry with same method, switch payment method, or downgrade plan.
Section 8

Visit Counting Rules

8.1 Definition of a Visit

Business Rule BL-010
A visit is defined as access to a single gym for an entire calendar day. One visit = unlimited access to the same gym for the full day. A morning session and an evening session at the same gym on the same day counts as 1 visit only.

8.2 Multi-Gym Same-Day Rules

Business Rule BL-011
Visiting a different gym on the same calendar day counts as an additional visit and depletes the monthly cap accordingly. Basic tier members are restricted to 1 gym per day (multi-gym same-day access is blocked). Pro, Premium, and Executive tier members may visit multiple gyms on the same day.
Example: Pro Pass member (30 visits/month) Mon: checks in at Gym A at 7am → 29 remaining Mon: checks in at Gym A again at 6pm → still 29 remaining (same gym, same day) Mon: checks in at Gym B at 8pm → 28 remaining (different gym, same day) Tue: checks in at Gym B → 27 remaining Example: Basic Pass member (20 visits/month, 1 gym/day) Mon: checks in at Gym A at 7am → 19 remaining Mon: checks in at Gym A at 6pm → still 19 remaining (same gym, same day) Mon: attempts check-in at Gym B → BLOCKED (Basic = 1 gym per day)

8.3 Visit Counter Reset

  • The monthly visit counter resets on the same calendar date as subscription start, not on the 1st of each month.
  • Example: Subscribed on 10th March → counter resets on 10th April.
  • Unused visits do not roll over to the following month.

8.4 Unlimited Visits (Executive)

Executive Pass holders have no visit count limit. Check-ins are still logged for analytics and gym payout purposes — the system must continue to record every check-in event. Executive members may visit multiple gyms per day with no restriction.

8.5 Visit Soft Caps

Tier Soft Cap (visits/month) Gyms per Day
Basic 20 1 gym only
Pro 30 Multiple
Premium 40 Multiple
Executive Unlimited Multiple

8.5 Direct Subscription Visits

Visits by direct subscription members to their home gym are not counted against any visit allowance — their access is governed solely by the terms of their direct subscription (unlimited for monthly, single-entry for daily). These visits are still logged for gym analytics and payout purposes.

Section 9

Payment Processing

9.1 Supported Payment Methods (MVP)

Method Provider Type Priority
M-Pesa Vodacom Tanzania Mobile Money Primary
Airtel Money Airtel Tanzania Mobile Money Primary
Selcom Selcom Africa Mobile Wallet Secondary
Bank Transfer CRDB, NMB, NBC, Stanbic Bank Secondary

9.2 Currency

All transactions are processed and displayed in Tanzanian Shilling (TZS). No foreign currency conversion is required at MVP. Future expansion to Kenya (KES) and Uganda (UGX) will require multi-currency support.

9.3 Transaction Flow

  1. Member selects plan/service and taps "Pay".
  2. App presents available payment methods.
  3. Member selects method and enters phone number (mobile money) or confirms bank details.
  4. FitFlex initiates payment request via payment gateway API (e.g. Selcom API, M-Pesa Daraja Tanzania).
  5. Member receives STK push or USSD prompt on their phone to confirm payment.
  6. On success: subscription/booking is activated immediately. Confirmation sent via push + SMS.
  7. On failure: member is shown recovery options and the transaction is not recorded.
ℹ️
Dev Note — Payment Gateway
Selcom's API is the recommended starting point for Tanzania as it aggregates M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, and bank payments under a single integration. Vodacom Tanzania's M-Pesa Daraja API is an alternative for direct M-Pesa integration. Both require a Tanzanian business registration and CBT licence compliance.
Section 10

Access Control & QR Check-In

10.1 Check-In Methods

Two check-in modes are supported, both equivalent in validity:

Mode Who Scans What Is Scanned Use Case
Member Scans Gym Member (phone camera) Gym's static QR code (posted at entrance) Self-service, no staff needed
Gym Scans Member Gym operator (camera in app) Member's dynamic QR code (in FitFlex app) Staffed gyms, higher security

10.2 Check-In Validation Logic

Business Rule BL-012
Before confirming a check-in, the system must verify all of the following in sequence:
  1. Is the member's subscription active (not expired, not in grace period with 0 hours remaining)?
  2. Does the member's subscription tier grant access to this gym's tier?
  3. If on a limited visit plan (Basic/Pro/Premium): does the member have remaining visits for this month? Has the member already visited a different gym today (Basic tier = 1 gym/day restriction)?
  4. Is the gym currently within its operating hours?

All four conditions must be TRUE for check-in to succeed. The failure message shown to the member must be specific to which condition failed.

10.3 Check-In Failure Messages

Failure Reason Message to Member Action Prompted
Subscription expired "Your subscription has expired." Renew Now button
Gym tier not covered by plan "This gym requires a [Pro/Premium/Executive] Pass." Upgrade Plan button
No visits remaining "You've used all [X] visits this month." Upgrade Plan or wait for reset
Gym closed "[Gym Name] is currently closed. Opens at [time]." None

10.4 Check-In Data Logged

Every successful check-in must log the following to the database:

  • Member user ID
  • Gym ID
  • Timestamp (date + time)
  • Check-in method (member-scanned or gym-scanned)
  • Subscription type used (pass tier / direct sub / top-up)
  • Visit number in current billing cycle (for capped plans)
