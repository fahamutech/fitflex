## What we took from Membroz, and what we did instead

| Membroz pattern | FitFlex version |
|---|---|
| One fixed template, every post | Our three templates on the weekly rotation (editorial → v2 → v3), so the grid is consistent but not monotonous |
| Message first, one outcome per post | Every cover is one claim in Swahili with English under it; one keyword CTA per post |
| Feature strip of four icons | One checklist slide per carousel, four rows, big type, no icons |
| Access control (face recognition device) as the lead feature | The real QR check-in that refreshes every minute, the owner dashboard and messaging; no device, no generated screens |
| AI-generated gyms, people and reels | Licensed Pexels photos (people never presented as members, trainers or partners) and text-motion reels on our own template; no AI imagery in November |
| "Welcome to the family" partner posts with logos and flags | Not in this plan. A partner post needs the gym's written consent and its own photos; add one the day a partner agrees |
| Demo CTA | Keyword CTA the owner answers by hand: PARTNER, TRAINER, PASS, plus two new ones this month, TEAM and VENDOR |

## Reels

Three text-motion reels (14, 22, 28 Nov), 1080×1920, about 19–21 s, silent, built by `social/tools/render-reel.js` from `source/reel.json`: the week's template renders the frames at 1080×1350, each frame is padded to 9:16 on its own ground colour, with a slow push-in and 0.4 s fades. Burned-in text, so they read without sound. Output `reel/<prefix>.mp4` plus a cover PNG.

Still to settle before the first reel is reserved:
- The Metricool connector has only been used for image carousels. The first reel reservation must confirm that a video URL with `instagramData.type = REEL` is accepted; if not, the owner posts the file by hand and we register it with `publish.js manual`.
- No music until a licence is recorded in the record's `licences.md`.
- Real app-screen reels (the kind the October plan wanted) still need a screen recording from the owner's phone or the simulator; `2026-10-W1-D-reel-members` stays an idea until then.
