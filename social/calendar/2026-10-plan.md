# FitFlex Instagram — Month 1 Content Plan (draft)

Decision (2026-10-09): the account speaks to **all audiences** — members, trainers, gym owners/partners, and (once clubs and corporates are in focus) clubs and companies.
Basis: `INSTAGRAM_SWIPE_FILE_2026-10-09.md`. This is a draft built from saved posts, not from performance data. Treat the mix as a hypothesis to test.

## Audience pillars

| Pillar | Audience | Job of the post | Main formats (from swipe file) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Find & train** | Members | Show how easy it is to find a gym or trainer | Short Swahili-first reels, technique tips, comment-keyword offers |
| **Grow your business** | Trainers | Show FitFlex brings clients and handles payouts | Positioning tips, trainer spotlights, "comment PLAN" funnels |
| **Run a better gym** | Gym owners / partners | Make owners trust FitFlex as the operator's tool | Data carousels, contrarian one-liners, partner-gym build/opening updates |
| **Community** | Clubs, groups, companies | Show challenges, groups and wellness programmes | Challenge recaps, leaderboard screenshots, "gym is a community" posts |
| **Marketplace** | Vendors | Show equipment and nutrition with price | Price-on-screen reels, vendor spotlights |

## Weekly rhythm (suggested, 4 posts a week)

| Day | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Run a better gym | Carousel: hook slide + 5–8 lesson/data slides + save ask |
| Wed | Find & train or Community | Reel, under 30s, Swahili-first caption, one line |
| Fri | Grow your business | Carousel or reel with a comment-keyword offer |
| Sat | Marketplace or Community | Price-on-screen reel, or challenge/event recap |

## Four-week themes

**Week 1 — Introduce the idea (all audiences)**
- Mon carousel: "Most gyms are not ready for what's coming" angle, written in FitFlex's own words and data (do not copy the original).
- Wed reel: "Find a gym near you in two taps" (member).
- Fri carousel: "3 things clients ask before they book a trainer" (trainer).
- Sat reel: first partner gym or vendor spotlight.

**Week 2 — Proof and local voice**
- Mon: contrarian one-liner carousel, e.g. why attendance data beats sign-up numbers.
- Wed: local trainer feature (with permission), Swahili caption.
- Fri: "Comment PLAN" offer — a free 3-day starter plan, delivered by DM.
- Sat: vendor price reel from the FitFlex marketplace.

**Week 3 — Community**
- Mon: owner carousel on community and retention.
- Wed: challenge launch reel.
- Fri: trainer positioning tips ("speak to the problem, not about yourself").
- Sat: group or company wellness recap.

**Week 4 — Build in public**
- Mon: carousel with what FitFlex shipped this month.
- Wed: behind-the-scenes reel from a partner gym.
- Fri: "Comment GYM" funnel — get the best gyms near you.
- Sat: month recap and next month's challenge.

## Funnels to set up before posting

1. **Comment-keyword automation.** fitflex.africa is a Business account (confirmed 2026-10-09), so the official Instagram API is available. Comment-to-DM works through the API's private-reply feature: a comment containing a keyword triggers one DM to that commenter. Setup needs a Meta developer app, the account connected to it, and permissions for comments, messaging and publishing. While the app is in development mode it only works for accounts with a role on the app, which covers our own account. Decide what each keyword delivers (GYM, PLAN, PARTNER). Verify current permission names and limits in Meta's docs before building.
2. **Link in bio.** One landing page with four paths: member app, trainer sign-up, gym partner sign-up, vendor sign-up.
3. **Tracking.** UTM links per path so sign-ups can be tied back to posts.

## Production with no budget (decided 2026-10-09)

No filming or editing budget, so content is AI-assisted and built from what FitFlex already owns.

| Content type | How it's made | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Carousels** (3 of 4 weekly posts) | Written by Claude, laid out in Gamma or Figma, exported as images | Cheapest, and carousels are the strongest saved format for gym owners and trainers |
| **App reels** | Screen recordings of the real FitFlex app (iOS Simulator or a phone), with text overlays and captions | Real product footage, shows the product honestly, no filming needed |
| **Text-motion reels** | Hook line, short animated text, brand colours | Works for contrarian one-liners and tips |
| **Illustrations and abstract visuals** | AI-generated images | Backgrounds, icons, scenes without identifiable people |

Avoid:
- AI-generated "members", "trainers" or "testimonials" presented as real. It breaks trust and Instagram's rules on labelling AI content.
- AI-generated gym footage passed off as a real partner gym.
- Equipment or prices shown that are not real.

Real people (partner gyms, trainers, vendors) get added gradually: ask for a short phone clip or photos and turn them into a post.

**Language:** alternate. Odd weeks Swahili-first captions, even weeks English-first, with a short second-language line on each post. Gym-owner carousels can stay English with a Swahili caption.

**Revised weekly mix:** 3 carousels + 1 reel per week (instead of 2 + 2). Reels are app screen recordings or text-motion.

## Rules

- Do not copy another account's wording, images or video. Study the structure and write original content.
- Get permission before featuring a trainer, gym or member.
- Do not claim prices, features or numbers the product does not support. Check with the live app and the legal drafts first.
- Keep the first month small enough to measure: after four weeks, compare saves, shares and DMs per pillar, then change the mix.
