## Carousel A — Gym owners (Monday)

**Angle:** the number that matters is who actually trains, not who signs up. Contrarian hook, original wording.

| # | Slide text |
|---|---|
| 1 | **You're counting the wrong members.** |
| 2 | Sign-ups tell you who joined. Visits tell you who stayed. |
| 3 | Most small gyms track visits in a notebook, or not at all. |
| 4 | When you can't see visits, you can't see who is about to leave. |
| 5 | Empty off-peak hours aren't a mystery. They're a data problem. |
| 6 | A digital dashboard shows check-ins, busy hours and who stopped coming. |
| 7 | And it brings new members to your door. |
| 8 | **Own a gym in Dar es Salaam?** Comment **PARTNER** and we'll DM you how FitFlex works. |

**Caption (Swahili-first):**
> Unahesabu wanachama wasiofaa? 👀

> *English: Sign-ups tell you who joined. Visits tell you who stayed. Comment PARTNER to learn how FitFlex works for gyms.*

## Carousel B — Trainers (Friday)

**Angle:** trainers lose clients to the same three problems. Positioning idea adapted in our own words: say who you help, not who you are.

| # | Slide text |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Why clients don't book you.** (It's not your price.) |
| 2 | 1. They can't tell what you actually help with. |
| 3 | "Personal trainer" says nothing. "I help desk workers get rid of back pain" does. |
| 4 | 2. They can't see proof. Photos, short clips and honest reviews build trust. |
| 5 | 3. Booking and paying is hard. Cash, DMs and "I'll send it later" kill sales. |
| 6 | Fix all three: a clear one-line offer, visible proof, and booking and payment in one place. |
| 7 | FitFlex gives trainers a profile, bookings and payment through the app. |
| 8 | **Are you a trainer?** Comment **TRAINER** and we'll DM you how to join. |

**Caption (Swahili-first):**
> Kwa nini wateja hawakubuki? Mara nyingi si bei.
> Sema unamsaidia nani na tatizo gani, onyesha ushahidi, na wape njia rahisi ya kulipa.
> Mkufunzi? Andika **TRAINER** kwenye comments tukutumie jinsi ya kujiunga.
> *English: Say who you help, show proof, make paying easy. Trainers, comment TRAINER to learn how to join.*

**Hashtags:** #personaltrainer #fitnesscoach #tanzania #daressalaam #fitflexafrica

**Check before posting:** confirm the trainer onboarding flow is open to new sign-ups and that reviews are visible on trainer profiles in the current app build.
