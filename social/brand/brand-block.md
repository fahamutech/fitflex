# Brand block for image prompts and brand review

Source of truth: `canon/04-marketing/design-system.md` (founder's workspace). Tokens: `brand-tokens.json` next to this file. Decision 2026-10-16: the brand primary is the app's green `#1F7A3A`; `#00B67A` is the on-dark brand text and verified tint, not the primary.

## Image-style block (append to every image-generation prompt)

> FitFlex Africa brand style: deep navy-slate background (#0B192C / #0F172A) or warm cream (#FFF6E5) for light pieces, forest-green primary (#1F7A3A) as the hero accent with amber (#FF9800) and gold (#FDBE34) highlights, soft coloured glow behind the focal point; clean modern fintech aesthetic; Inter-style clean type; generous rounded corners (24–44 px); authentic Dar es Salaam / Tanzanian setting; premium, energetic, high contrast. [ASPECT: 1:1 | 4:5 | 9:16 | 16:9]. No text baked into the image; no distorted hands; no logos other than FitFlex.

## Do

- Social slides (template v2, owner 2026-10-20): deep slate `#020617` ground, Inter 900 headline with one word in Volt `#C8F04A`, JetBrains Mono eyebrow, Fuel `#FF8A6B` only for an "act now" row, photos under a dark scrim. Volt never on a light ground; green `#1F7A3A` for any light piece.

- Build on navy or cream; put green, amber and gold on those grounds.
- Use the role colour when a post targets one audience: member green `#1F7A3A`, owner amber `#FF9800`, trainer blue `#3B82F6`, vendor purple `#A855F7`, corporate indigo `#6366F1`.
- Inter for headlines and body (weights up to 900), JetBrains Mono for stats and labels.
- FitFlex logo on the first or last slide only; clear space equals the pin width; the "Af" in the wordmark stays gold `#FDBE34`.
- Show real Tanzanian settings, mobile money, phones, real gyms.
- One idea per slide; a hook on slide 1; a save or comment ask on the last slide.
- Every caption bilingual; Swahili first on odd weeks, English first on even weeks.

## Don't

- Brand colour on white, logo gradient as a fill, stretched or shadowed logos.
- Invented testimonials, quotes, member counts, prices or payout percentages. Numbers come from the glossary with their tag.
- Stock-photo people presented as FitFlex members, trainers or partners.
- Copying another account's wording, images or video. Study the structure, write original content.
- "Cheapest" or savings claims. FitFlex is flexibility, verified quality, one app, mobile money.
- Promising iOS, prices or payouts in a DM or caption while the app and legal texts lag.

## Voice

Confident, energetic, locally grounded. Concrete specifics (TSh, M-Pesa, Dar es Salaam) over wellness fluff. Address the reader as "you"; the brand is "FitFlex".
