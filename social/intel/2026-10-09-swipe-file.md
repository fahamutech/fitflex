## 3. Patterns worth copying

1. **Comment-keyword funnel.** The caption says "Comment WORD and I'll send you X". It converts engagement into DMs and a lead list. FitFlex version: "Comment GYM to get the best gyms near you" or "Comment PLAN for a free 3-day starter".
2. **Operator-authority carousels.** Big hook slide, then 5–10 data or lesson slides, then a save/share ask. Aimed at gym owners and trainers, they feed the B2B and partner pipeline.
3. **Price-on-screen vendor reels.** Local dealers show the product, the spec and a TZS price. Fits the marketplace and vendor module.
4. **Build-in-public.** @tanzanite.sportshub posts construction progress. FitFlex can do the same with partner gym openings and new-feature drops.
5. **Community as the product.** @kleosfitness frames the gym as a culture, not equipment. Fits FitFlex groups, challenges and clubs.
6. **Short, local, Swahili-first captions.** The strongest local voices mix Swahili and English and keep captions to one line.
7. **Contrarian one-liners.** "Most gyms are not ready…", "The gym is the new nightlife." Strong hooks for a first slide.
8. **Technique teaching with numbered steps.** Low production, high trust. Good trainer-account content that FitFlex can resurface.
