# Platform specs for FitFlex social content

Sizes and limits as commonly published by each network as of October 2026. Re-check the network's own help pages before a format is used for the first time; limits change. Instagram and the FitFlex Africa Facebook Page are the networks selected for publishing (owner, 2026-10-16); TikTok and LinkedIn are here so variants can be drafted.

| Platform | Format | Size (px) | Copy limits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram and Facebook Page | Carousel (feed) | 1080×1350 (4:5) or 1080×1080 | Caption up to 2,200 chars; first ~125 shown; max 30 hashtags, we use 5 | 2–20 slides; first slide carries the hook; "Swipe →" footer on all but the last |
| Instagram | Reel | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Caption as above | Keep the hook in the first 2 s; captions burned in for silent viewing; cover frame at 4:5 safe area |
| Instagram | Story | 1080×1920 | Short text only | Keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px |
| TikTok | Video | 1080×1920 | Caption up to 2,200 chars, keep under 150; 3–5 hashtags | Vertical, hook in 1–2 s, native text overlays preferred; no FitFlex account recorded yet |
| Facebook Page | Image post / carousel | 1080×1350 or 1200×630 | Short copy, link allowed | FitFlex Africa Page connected in Metricool 2026-10-16; same carousel as Instagram |
| LinkedIn | Document post (PDF carousel) | 1080×1350 or 1920×1080 | Up to 3,000 chars; first ~210 shown | Best fit for the gym-owner and corporate pillars; English lead; Company Page not confirmed |
| LinkedIn | Image post | 1200×627 | As above | |

## Variant rules

- One content record, one brief, one block per platform under "Platform variants". A platform the owner has not confirmed is marked `not adapted`, never silently published.
- Language: odd weeks Swahili-first, even weeks English-first, every caption carries both. LinkedIn is English-first regardless.
- Keyword CTAs (PASS, PARTNER, TRAINER) only where a reply path exists: Instagram comments once the comment-to-DM module ships, otherwise a manual reply is noted in the brief.
- Hashtags: 5 on Instagram, 3 to 5 on TikTok, 3 on LinkedIn, none required on Facebook.
- Never promise iOS, prices or payout percentages in any variant while the app and the legal texts lag canon.
